# About *Star Stuff*

Zines and field guides on the science of belonging — we are made of star stuff, and we belong not despite our differences but as them.

---

Star Stuff (starstuff.earth) is a collaboration between the [Stimpunks Foundation](https://stimpunks.org/) and [More Realms](https://morerealms.com/) — a growing collection of zines and field guides that read settled science as an argument about belonging. It is the public flowering of the Stimpunks knowledge garden: if stimpunks.org is the garden, this is where it blooms. And a great deal of what blooms here was grown from seed in More Realms.

The method is consistent. Each piece begins with something well-established in physics, cosmology, chemistry, or biology, and lets it carry a claim about neurodivergent and disabled life — not as loose metaphor, but as the same pattern showing up at more than one scale. Almost every atom heavier than helium was forged in stars and their deaths; the calcium in our bones and the iron in our blood came from stars that died before the Earth existed. We take that settled science and read it as an argument: if we are all made of the same stellar matter, then neurodivergent and disabled people belong here fully — as part of the universe knowing itself in every possible way.

## An official collaboration

Stimpunks Foundation × More Realms. Two sites, one argument: difference comes first.

[Stimpunks](https://stimpunks.org/) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit built by and for neurodivergent and disabled people — a knowledge garden of glossary, patterns, and practice. [More Realms](https://morerealms.com/) is **Helen Edgar**'s speculative companion to her [Autistic Realms](https://autisticrealms.com/): where Autistic Realms is the grounded, practical site, More Realms is, in her words, “looser, weirder, more speculative” — somewhere to wander and think sideways rather than conclude. Star Stuff is what the two of us make together, and the pairing is on every page for that reason.

Helen's writing is not a footnote to this collection — it is one of its sources and much of its weather. *Why Difference Comes First*, *We Are Made of Relations*, *My Monotropic Galaxy*, the shared-air and shared-signal coinages, the constellation reading of the spiky profile: these arrived here from More Realms and Autistic Realms, and they set the frame that later pieces work inside. Three zines are hers outright, in her own voice — Nos. [11](https://starstuff.earth/why-difference-comes-first-zine.html), [28](https://starstuff.earth/wyrd-and-weird-zine.html), and [30](https://starstuff.earth/always-migrating-zine.html) — one more, No. [29](https://starstuff.earth/only-in-relation-zine.html), was developed after her essay, and her work is named and cited across much of the rest.

Attribution stays specific underneath the co-branding. Where a piece is Helen's, it says so and it is hers. Where a piece takes an idea from her, it names the essay. The two organizations share the masthead; the individual credits stay exact.

How the three sites actually feed each other — which essays became which zines, which glossary terms became which arguments, and why a web of linked notes produces concepts nobody wrote down — is its own page: [Cosmic Connections](https://starstuff.earth/cosmic-connections.html).

## The through-line

Difference is not a deviation from a norm. It is the default state of matter — and we belong as it, not despite it.

That claim gets made across scales, and the scales are the point. The same shape keeps turning up whether you look through a telescope, a microscope, or a front door.

The cosmic & subatomic

Relativity and crip time; phase transitions and fluctuating capacity; spectroscopy and the constellation that was never a severity scale; the near-uniform early universe that needed tiny differences to make any structure at all.

The ecological & biological

Niche construction and worldmaking; the umwelt and the double empathy problem; symbiosis and the self that was always a collective; tides and co-regulation.

The lived & communal

Monotropism and spiral time; threshold anxiety and the crossing; functioning labels and the constellation of intensities; memory, mutual aid, and the work of being loved down to your star stuff.

## How it's made, and held

Each piece pairs a real diagram and honest sourcing with the community's own knowledge, and many are built with — or directly credit — neurodivergent thinkers. Everything is written in the first person plural: *we*, not *they*. And everything is [held in common](https://stimpunks.org/commons/) under Creative Commons, made to be printed free and shared freely. The zines are flowers of the garden, and a flower's whole purpose is to give its seeds away.

A note on the science

These are resonances, not proofs. The physics does not owe us our ethics, and we don't pretend it does — we begin from well-established science and read it as an argument about belonging, noticing where the same shape recurs. Where a source is contested or an interpretation is one among several, we say so. **We are noticing a rhyme, not collecting a proof.**

Sources & corrections

We trace every quotation to a primary source, credit coinages to the people who made them, and ground the science in real, checkable work. We're human, and the collection is living — fact-checking here is ongoing, not finished. Every audit and every correction is published in the [changelog](https://starstuff.earth/changelog.html), our own errors included. If you spot an error or a misattribution, please tell us: open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Stimpunks/Star-Stuff/issues) or reach us at [stimpunks.org](https://stimpunks.org), and we'll fix it. Solidarity, not charity — corrections are mutual aid.

We love you down to your star stuff.
